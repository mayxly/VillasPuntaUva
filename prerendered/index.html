Location

A 5-Minute Walk To Punta Uva's Best Beach

Most villas sit just a 5-minute walk from Playa Arrecife and Punta Uva Beach—recently ranked among the World's 50 Best Beaches and the crown jewel of the Puerto Viejo coast. Tucked away in a peaceful spot just off the main road. The property requires no 4x4 vehicle to reach restaurants, sodas, and the local stores as they are all within easy walking distance.
